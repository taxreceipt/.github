# Security policy

tax.receipt builds verification tools for insurance claims, so we take security reports seriously.

## How to report

Email **hello@taxreceipt.io** with "Security" in the subject line. Include:

- what you found and where (URL, page or repository);
- the steps to reproduce it;
- any impact you have observed.

Please do not open a public issue, and please do not share details publicly until we have had a chance to look.

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report and tell you who is handling it.
- We will tell you what we find and what we plan to do.
- We will not take action against anyone who reports a problem in good faith and follows this policy.

## Please do not

- access, change or delete data that is not yours;
- run tests that could disrupt a service (for example denial-of-service or bulk automated scanning);
- use social engineering against our people, partners or suppliers.

## Scope

Our public demo uses fictional data only. No real claimant, patient, bank, clinic or insurer data is held in it.
